ABOUT THE EVENT

Built in 72 hours — made to last a decade.

The Vision 2030 Youth Innovation Cup brought together 1,248 builders from universities, bootcamps, and high schools across Saudi Arabia for a three-day sprint aimed at one goal: ship working prototypes against six national priority tracks.

Participants worked alongside 42 industry mentors from leading organizations and pitched final submissions to a cross-sector panel — with top teams receiving direct pathways into accelerator programs and government innovation funds.
