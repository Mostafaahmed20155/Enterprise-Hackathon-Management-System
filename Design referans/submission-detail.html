Description

EN · AR available

Transit Accessibility Navigator is a routing app for wheelchair users, parents with strollers, and travellers with limited mobility. It surfaces real-time elevator outages, tactile paths, and step-free transfers across the Riyadh Metro network.

The app combines live station-equipment feeds from the operator with a citizen-reporting layer (crowd-sourced outage reports, verified within 10 minutes by our volunteer stewards). Routes are scored on accessibility confidence, not just travel time.

We ran a 3-week pilot with 42 participants across 6 stations, covering both peak and off-peak journeys. Participants reported a 38% reduction in disrupted transfers and a 2.4-point lift in confidence scores on the WHOQOL mobility scale.
